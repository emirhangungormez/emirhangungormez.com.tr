02 — Why?

Kuran23 is a modern study platform reimagining reading, researching, and reflecting upon the Quran.

The vast majority of digital Quran systems focus solely on reading. Comparing verses across different translations, accessing reliable tafsirs, cross-referencing hadiths, taking notes, and conducting research often require context-switching across multiple apps or websites.

Kuran23 was developed to unify this fragmented experience under one roof. Instead of just reading a single translation, users can compare translations side-by-side, access classical tafsirs, inspect hadith collections, create personal workspaces, and engage in reflective conversations with Muallim AI.

Our long-term goal is not just to build a content server; Kuran23 is a modern study platform redesigning reading, research, reflection, and learning for the digital age.

03 — What I Built

Multi-Source Experience

An intelligent AI guide and RAG architecture built around "What are you curious about?" to aid reflection on the Quran.

04 — Architecture & Learnings
01 — EDGE INFRASTRUCTURE

Cloudflare Edge Architecture

Low-latency, high-performance global access built on Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Edge Functions.

02 — SEARCH ENGINE

Semantic Content Discovery

Smart search offering intent-driven content discovery across translations, tafsirs, hadiths, and books rather than simple keyword matching.

03 — AI ARCHITECTURE

RAG & Muallim AI Guide

Quran-focused Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture empowering Muallim AI to assist deep verse reflection.

04 — PRODUCT LESSON

More Features ≠ Better Experience

Users prefer simple, fast experiences over complex menus. Sub-millisecond speed builds immediate trust.

05 — DESIGN

Power of Comparative Reading

Seeing different translations side-by-side elevates learning; personal notes and collections drive engagement.

06 — AI VISION

Muallim is a Guide

Muallim's goal is not to give direct answers, but to facilitate the user's own research, reflection, and inquiry.
